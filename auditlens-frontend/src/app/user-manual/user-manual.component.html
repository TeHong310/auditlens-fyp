Purpose: submit invoice documents to AuditLens for OCR extraction and audit review.

Workflow

{{ step }}
  • Go to Upload Document and choose a PDF or image (JPG/PNG) of the invoice.
  • AuditLens accepts a scanned copy, a phone photo, or a digitally-generated PDF — no special formatting needed.
  • After upload, the document is automatically OCR/AI-extracted, then moves to OCR Review for you to confirm, and finally becomes visible to auditors for three-way matching and audit processing.
Open Upload Document

A Transaction Package groups documents that belong to the same procurement case, so an auditor sees them together instead of as separate, disconnected uploads. A package can contain more than one invoice — e.g. when a single Purchase Order is billed across several invoices.

Document relationships

Invoice Purchase Order (PO) Goods Receipt (GR)

Examples

  • Single invoice package — one invoice grouped on its own.
  • Multiple invoice package — several invoices billed against the same order, grouped together.
  • Invoice + PO + GR package — the full three-way set for one procurement case.
Open Transactions

Purpose: review the invoice information AuditLens extracted automatically before it goes to audit.

Fields to check

  • Vendor
  • Invoice number
  • Invoice date
  • Amount
  • Line items

What to do

  • Review each extracted field against the original document.
  • Correct any value OCR read incorrectly.
  • Confirm the information once it's accurate.
Open OCR Review
  • An auditor sends a document back here when something needs your attention — a missing document, an unclear field, or a value that needs verifying.
  • Open the case to see the auditor's exact reason and requested action.
  • Upload the missing document or correct the flagged information, then respond so the auditor can review it again.
Open Correction Center
  • Reports summarize your submitted documents and their current status at a glance.
  • Use them to monitor what's pending, what was sent back, and what still needs follow-up, without opening every record individually.
Open Reports

Purpose: your at-a-glance overview of the audit workload and system-wide risk signals, shown when you log in.

Charts on this page

  • Audit Decision Trend — daily Approved / Need Review / Sent Back counts over the last 7 days.
  • Review Workload — how many transactions are Pending Review, Need Review, Completed, or Sent Back right now.
  • Status Breakdown — the Pass / Review / Missing Document split across all transactions.
  • Authenticity Outcomes — the Pass / Warning / Fail split of authenticity checks.
  • Audit Risk Distribution — how many transactions currently sit at High, Medium, or Low risk.
  • Top Risk Suppliers — the 5 suppliers with the most risk-related transactions, so a recurring problem vendor stands out at a glance.
Open Auditor Dashboard

Purpose: see every transaction assigned for your review in one prioritized list.

  • Prioritization — cases needing attention surface first, instead of a plain chronological list.
  • Status filtering — narrow the queue to a specific stage of review.
  • Risk filtering — narrow the queue to a specific risk level.
  • Pagination — results show 10 records per page, with page controls below the table.
  • Click any row to open its full transaction detail.
Open Review Queue

Purpose: see every transaction's Invoice / PO / GR match verdict in one list, without opening each record individually.

  • Filter by match status — All, Passed, Review Required, or Failed.
  • Search by invoice number or supplier.
  • Pagination — results show 10 records per page, with page controls below the table.
  • Click View Details on any row to see the full Invoice/PO/GR comparison.
Open Three-Way Matching

Common exceptions

  • Missing documents — the PO or GR hasn't been uploaded yet.
  • Matching issues — a field (vendor, amount, PO reference, line items) doesn't agree across documents.
  • Risk findings — an anomaly or authenticity concern was detected.
  • Open the exception to see exactly what's wrong and why, using the record's Exception Summary and Field Comparison.
  • Document your findings in the Remarks/Notes field before approving or sending the case back.
Open Exceptions
  • Purpose: check whether a document's visual evidence (logo, company name, stamp, signature) matches what a genuine document from that vendor should show.
  • Verification results — each check returns Passed or Warning, with the specific evidence it found or couldn't find.
  • Risk interpretation — a Warning is advisory, not a verdict of fraud — use it alongside the rest of the case to decide whether to verify further with the vendor.
  • Pagination — the document list shows 10 records per page, with page controls below it.
Open Authenticity

Anomaly checks run automatically whenever a document is processed — this page is a read-only view of the results, there's no manual "Run Analysis" button to press.

Common anomaly types

  • Amount anomaly — the invoice amount is unusually high compared to this vendor's history.
  • Round amount — the invoice total is a suspiciously round figure (e.g. exactly RM 5,000.00).
  • Weekend transaction — the invoice is dated on a weekend, unusual for typical business activity.
  • Duplicate invoice — this invoice closely matches another already in the system.

Status filters

  • Pending — not yet reviewed or dismissed (the default view).
  • Reviewed — you've looked into it and taken it into account.
  • Dismissed — you've determined it isn't a real concern.
