Open Finance Home

Purpose: submit invoice, PO, or GR documents to AuditLens for OCR extraction and audit review.

Workflow

{{ step }}
  • Drag files onto the dropzone or click Browse Files — PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, or a ZIP of these, up to 10MB each.
  • AuditLens tries to detect each file's document type automatically. If it can't, Confirm Document Types asks you to pick Invoice, Purchase Order, or Goods Receipt before it continues.
  • Once confirmed, the file is automatically OCR/AI-extracted, then moves to OCR Review for you to check, and finally becomes visible to auditors for three-way matching.
Open Upload Document

Purpose: group documents that belong to the same procurement case, so an auditor sees them together instead of as separate, disconnected uploads.

Document relationships

Invoice Purchase Order (PO) Goods Receipt (GR)
  • Click New Transaction Package, name it, then upload its PO(s), invoice(s), and GR(s) — each into its own upload zone — and click Create Package.
  • The package list shows every package's supplier, document count, status, and created date — click a row to open it, or Delete to remove it.
  • A package's detail page shows its documents plus a Relationship Preview — a read-only view of how they link together (matching itself happens automatically once Enterprise Matching runs).
Open Transactions

Purpose: review the information AuditLens extracted automatically — across Invoices, Purchase Orders, and Goods Receipts — before it goes to audit.

  • Three tabs — Invoice, Purchase Order, and Goods Receipt — each with its own review list.
  • The summary row shows how many documents Need Correction, are Waiting Review, or are Ready to Submit.
  • For invoices, the PO Status/GR Status columns show whether a matching PO or GR has been linked yet.

Fields to check

  • Vendor
  • Invoice/PO/GR number
  • Date
  • Amount
  • Line items

What to do

  • Click Review to open a document and check each extracted field against the original.
  • Correct any value OCR read incorrectly, then confirm it — or Open to view the original file, or Delete to remove it.
Open OCR Review
  • An auditor sends a document back here when something needs your attention — a missing document, an unclear field, or a value that needs verifying.
  • The list shows each invoice's Priority, Return Reason, Required Action, and current Status — a red Overdue badge appears if the response is past due.
  • Open a row for its full Correction Detail page — the original invoice information alongside the auditor's exact instruction and due date.
  • Upload the missing document or correct the flagged information, then respond so the auditor can review it again.
Open Correction Center
  • KPI cards — Transaction Packages, Approved, Returned for Correction, Under Auditor Review, and Average OCR Confidence.
  • Transaction Status Distribution, Processing Trend (last 14 days), and Top Vendors by Transaction Volume charts summarize activity at a glance.
  • The Transaction Package Report table lists every package with its Match Status, Audit Status, and latest auditor remark — click View to open one.
  • Export CSV downloads the currently filtered report.
Open Reports

Purpose: your at-a-glance overview of the audit workload and system-wide risk signals, shown when you log in.

Charts on this page

  • Audit Decision Trend — daily Approved / Need Review / Sent Back counts over the last 7 days.
  • Review Workload — how many transactions are Pending Review, Need Review, Completed, or Sent Back right now.
  • Status Breakdown — the Pass / Review / Missing Document split across all transactions.
  • Authenticity Outcomes — the Pass / Warning / Fail split of authenticity checks.
  • Audit Risk Distribution — how many transactions currently sit at High, Medium, or Low risk.
  • Top Risk Suppliers — the 5 suppliers with the most risk-related transactions, so a recurring problem vendor stands out at a glance.
Open Auditor Dashboard

Purpose: see every transaction assigned for your review in one prioritized list.

  • Prioritization — cases needing attention surface first, instead of a plain chronological list.
  • Status filtering — narrow the queue to a specific stage of review.
  • Risk filtering — narrow the queue to a specific risk level.
  • Pagination — results show 10 records per page, with page controls below the table.
  • Click any row to open its full transaction detail.
Open Review Queue

Purpose: see every transaction's Invoice / PO / GR match verdict in one list, without opening each record individually.

  • Filter by match status — All, Passed, Review Required, or Failed.
  • Search by invoice number or supplier.
  • Pagination — results show 10 records per page, with page controls below the table.
  • Click View Details on any row to see the full Invoice/PO/GR comparison.
Open Three-Way Matching

Common exceptions

  • Missing documents — the PO or GR hasn't been uploaded yet.
  • Matching issues — a field (vendor, amount, PO reference, line items) doesn't agree across documents.
  • Risk findings — an anomaly or authenticity concern was detected.
  • Open the exception to see exactly what's wrong and why, using the record's Exception Summary and Field Comparison.
  • Document your findings in the Remarks/Notes field before approving or sending the case back.
Open Exceptions
  • Purpose: check whether a document's visual evidence (logo, company name, stamp, signature) matches what a genuine document from that vendor should show.
  • Verification results — each check returns Passed or Warning, with the specific evidence it found or couldn't find.
  • Risk interpretation — a Warning is advisory, not a verdict of fraud — use it alongside the rest of the case to decide whether to verify further with the vendor.
  • Pagination — the document list shows 10 records per page, with page controls below it.
Open Authenticity

Anomaly checks run automatically whenever a document is processed — this page is a read-only view of the results, there's no manual "Run Analysis" button to press.

Common anomaly types

  • Amount anomaly — the invoice amount is unusually high compared to this vendor's history.
  • Round amount — the invoice total is a suspiciously round figure (e.g. exactly RM 5,000.00).
  • Weekend transaction — the invoice is dated on a weekend, unusual for typical business activity.
  • Duplicate invoice — this invoice closely matches another already in the system.

Severity

High Risk

Requires investigation.

Medium Risk

Requires verification.

Low Risk

Informational context.

Status filters

  • Pending — not yet reviewed or dismissed (the default view).
  • Reviewed — you've looked into it and taken it into account.
  • Dismissed — you've determined it isn't a real concern.
Open Anomaly Detection
  • Explain Exception — understand detected issues in plain language.
  • Explain Risk — understand the risk impact of this case.
  • Approval Assessment — evaluate approval readiness before the auditor decides.
  • Generate Audit Remark — assist with audit documentation.
  • Prepare Send Back Instruction — help communicate required corrections to Finance.

AI supports auditor judgement. AI does NOT automatically approve transactions.

Open AI Audit Assistant

Available inside each record's Audit Decision panel — open any record from the queue to reach it.

Purpose: every record moves through the same Document Workflow Timeline, shown on its Record Detail page.

{{ step }}

Steps

  • Document Uploaded — the initial submission; shows the upload timestamp.
  • OCR Extraction — AuditLens reads the document automatically; shows the OCR confidence score.
  • Three-Way Matching — Invoice/PO/GR verification; shows the matching status plus who reviewed it and when. Use View Matching Details to see the full comparison.
  • Exception Review — review any detected discrepancies; shows the exception status. Use View Exception Details to investigate.
  • Authenticity Review — verify the document's authenticity signals; shows the authenticity status. Use View Authenticity Review to see the evidence.
  • Anomaly Review — review any detected anomalies; shows the anomaly status. Use View Anomaly Details for the full findings.
  • Final Audit Decision — your Approve / Send Back / Need Review decision; shows the final status, your name, and the decision timestamp.

Three-Way Matching through Anomaly Review unlock in order — mark each step Reviewed to move on to the next.
